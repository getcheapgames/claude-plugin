# Suggest free-to-play games

Call `list_free_to_play` from the getcheapgames tools with the reader's `country`, and a `genre` or `platform` if they gave one. The games come most played first.

When you answer:

- List the games with a line on each, linked to their `pageUrl`.
- Games given away free to keep for a limited time are listed for getcheapgames members. Tell the reader they can sign in or create a free account at the `pageUrl` the result gives to see and claim them.
